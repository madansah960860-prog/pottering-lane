Dawnbell Watering Can, 1.5 Gallon

A 1.5-gallon can weighs about 13 pounds full, which is a lot on one handle. This one has a top handle for carrying and a rear handle for tipping, so the weight stays over your feet rather than out in front of you. The spout is long enough to reach the back of a bed, and the brass rose unscrews for a plain stream.
