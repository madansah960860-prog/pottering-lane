Easygrip Hand Tool Set, 4 Piece

Four stainless heads on four identical handles, so whichever one you pick up feels the same in the hand. The grip is 1.4 inches around — considerably fatter than a standard trowel — with a thumb rest moulded into the top and a wrist strap through the end. Depth marks are stamped into the trowel and transplanter blades.
