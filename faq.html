Shipping

We ship your order within 1–3 business days of receiving it. That is a commitment, not an average. After it ships, standard delivery takes 5–8 business days and expedited takes 2–3 business days — those are the carrier’s transit times.

Standard shipping is $7.95, and it is free on orders over $75. Expedited shipping is $16.95 on any order. There are no handling fees, no oversize fees and no surcharges — the raised planter ships at the same rate as a pair of gloves. Sales tax is calculated at checkout from your delivery address.

The United States only, by USPS and UPS. We do not ship internationally, to APO/FPO addresses, or oversized items to PO Boxes. Full detail is in the Shipping Policy.

If we find we cannot ship within 1–3 business days we contact you before that deadline, give you a definite new shipping date, and offer you the choice of waiting or cancelling for a full refund. If we cannot give a firm date, or the delay is more than 30 days, we cancel and refund unless you tell us otherwise. This is required by the Federal Trade Commission’s Mail, Internet, or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule and we follow it.

Returns

45 days from the day it is delivered, unused and in its original packaging. Email hello@potteringlane.com for a return number before you send anything back. We give longer than most shops because a tool bought in winter may not be used until spring.

If you have changed your mind, you do. If the item arrived damaged, faulty, or is not what you ordered, we do — we send a prepaid label and you are not out of pocket.

We inspect returns within 2 business days of arrival and refund to your original payment method within 5–10 business days of that. Your bank may then take a few days to show it. If you paid by cash equivalent we refund within seven working days, as the FTC rule requires.

Gloves worn in the garden and hoses or cans that have been filled cannot come back, for hygiene and resale reasons, and neither can anything assembled with the fixings used. Nothing else is excluded. The full list is in the Refund & Return Policy.

Accounts and privacy

We use your name, address and email to fulfil the order and to contact you about it. We do not sell or share personal information. Order records are kept for seven years for tax purposes; newsletter addresses are kept until you unsubscribe. Full detail, including your California rights, is in the Privacy Policy.

Use the unsubscribe link in any message, or email hello@potteringlane.com and ask. We act on it within ten business days and we never require anything beyond your email address.
